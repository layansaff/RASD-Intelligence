# RASD Intelligence

Enterprise Risk Intelligence & Business Continuity Platform

## Project Vision
Build an intelligent platform that helps organizations identify operational risks, understand critical dependencies, and improve business continuity.

## Core Objectives
- Identify and assess operational risks.
- Map dependencies between business processes, systems, and suppliers.
- Analyze the impact of service disruptions.
- Support response planning and recovery tracking.
- Use AI to extract information from documents and support risk analysis.

## Initial Use Case
Analyze how an external supplier outage affects a critical business process, identify potential risks, and track response actions.

## Technology Stack
- Python
- FastAPI
- PostgreSQL
- React
- TypeScript

## Project Status
Initial setup and requirements definition.

## Disclaimer
This is a prototype for learning and demonstration. Risk assessments and recommendations require human validation.
